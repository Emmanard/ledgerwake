# Governance

Ledgerwake begins as a maintainer-led open-source project.

- Product and architecture decisions are recorded in issues or ADRs.
- Maintainers have final responsibility for releases, security response, scope, and repository access.
- Maintainer status is earned through sustained, high-quality contribution, review, operational judgment, and security awareness—not contribution count or reward participation.
- Conflicts of interest, self-dealing, manufactured issues, plagiarism, harassment, and reward manipulation are prohibited.
- Security fixes may be developed privately and disclosed after users can upgrade.

The governance model will be revisited after the project has multiple sustained contributors.

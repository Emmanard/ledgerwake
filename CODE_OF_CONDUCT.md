# Code of Conduct

Ledgerwake is committed to a respectful, harassment-free open-source community.

Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims critically, respect privacy, disclose conflicts of interest, and focus feedback on work rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, plagiarism, malicious code, reward manipulation, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`.
